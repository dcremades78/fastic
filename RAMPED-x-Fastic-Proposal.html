01 · What we found

What we saw when we looked at Fastic on social.

Before writing anything, we looked at how Fastic shows up today and what people in Brazil and Spain actually search. Four things stood out.

A big product

10M+ downloads, and a 4.8 on the App Store.

Fastic is a fasting app with a timer, an AI food scanner and a Body Status that shows when the body tips into fat-burning. The Android app has passed 10M installs at a 4.4 rating across 421K reviews, and the App Store rating sits at 4.8. The product is already live in Portuguese and Spanish, so there is nothing to localise.

The paid lane is closed

TikTok won't let you buy ads for a fasting app.

TikTok's advertising policy bans ads for fasting apps and limits weight-loss ads. So the one thing that sells fasting best, a real person talking to camera, can't be reached by buying it. It has to be earned through creators. That is the gap this shortlist fills.

The search is already there

People look up fasting on TikTok every day.

"Jejum intermitente" in Brazil and "ayuno intermitente" in Spain each have their own TikTok discover pages and a steady stream of videos. The questions repeat: what breaks a fast, what to eat, how to start. A creator who answers them with Fastic turns a search into an install.

No single fasting face

Fasting lives inside nutrition and fitness feeds.

There is no one big 'fasting creator' in either market. The audience follows nutritionists, dietitians and weight-loss coaches who bring fasting up inside their normal content. Fastic wins by being the app those trusted voices actually use on camera.

Sources: Google Play listing for Fastic (installs, rating, features); App Store listing (rating); TikTok on its ad policy for fasting and weight-loss, as covered by Engadget; TikTok discover pages for jejum intermitente. Checked November 2026.

04 · A wildcard worth testing

Four creator types nobody expects from a fasting app.

Next to the shortlist, we'd keep a small part of the first wave for four creator types outside the usual nutrition crowd. A few creators each, measured like the rest. If one works, it becomes a channel of its own.

Wildcard 1

An animated character

Illustrators who build a recurring character fighting the 11am hunger, watching the clock down to the eating window and winning. Telling the fast as a weekly story keeps it far from before-and-after hype. It is a format that worked very well for us with Flo.

A week in the life of one character, counting down to the eating window.
#animation · #animatedseries · #illustration
Wildcard 2

Break the fast with me

Cooking, restaurant and grocery creators scan the first meal that breaks their fast with Fastic before they eat. It is the app's best five seconds, shown in a moment everyone knows.

"I scanned the meal that broke my fast. Here's what it said."
#whatieatinaday · #receitas · #mealprep
Wildcard 3

Focus, not weight loss

Productivity and gym creators who frame fasting as mental clarity and discipline, not a diet. It opens a male audience that the usual weight-loss content never reaches.

"I fasted 16:8 for 30 days. Here's what it did to my focus."
#productivity · #discipline · #mensfitness
Wildcard 4

The comedy of hunger

Comedians with a sketch habit: the staring contest with the clock, the 11am hunger, the first bite when the window opens. Humour breaks diet-ad fatigue and travels far.

"Me, watching the clock until my eating window finally opens."
#comedy · #relatable · #fastingproblems
How we'd test it: two or three creators per wildcard inside the first wave, each with their own link, so you can compare results against the main shortlist. We'd scout these creators after our call.
05 · A first wave

How a first wave would work.

Start small, measure installs per creator and keep only what works. We'd agree budget and timing together after a call, once we know what matters most to you.

1

Brief 5–8 creators per market

Picked from the list above, across sizes. Each one gets a brief, a unique link or code and the hooks to test.

2

Read installs per creator

After two to three weeks: installs, cost per install and the winning hook in each market. Tracked with your MMP or with codes and links.

3

Scale what works

Boost the best videos as Spark Ads, re-brief the creators who convert and swap the rest. The next wave starts from proof.
